Hardware¶

The BNO08x's package, pins, host-interface straps, supplies, timing and reference circuits, as the driver uses them. The three chips share one package and one pinout; they differ in firmware features only (Ambiguities).

Package and pins¶

A 28-pin LGA, body 5.2 x 3.8 mm, nominal height 1.1 mm, moisture sensitivity level 1, lead-free reflow up to 260 °C (data sheet section 7). Pin 1 is at the top right of the top view and the numbers run counter-clockwise.

The BNO08x seen from the top, in its LGA-28 package. Pin 1 is marked by the dot; the numbers run counter-clockwise from it. Each pad is coloured by the pin's electrical type (legend below). Next to it is the pin's name and, in mono, the hook of the driver's port that drives or reads it; a pin without a hook is not used by the driver.

Choosing the host interface¶

Two strap pins, PS1 and PS0, and BOOTN are sampled at reset and choose the host interface. The driver does not detect it: the port you write names the transport (kTransport) and the wiring must agree.

PS1 PS0 Host interface Driver transport
0 0 I2C Transport::I2c
0 1 UART-RVC Transport::UartRvc
1 0 UART with SHTP Transport::UartShtp
1 1 SPI Transport::Spi

BOOTN must be high at reset for the application to start; it needs a 10 kΩ pull-up (a low level starts the bootloader, which this driver does not support). SPI needs both strap pins high from before reset until after the first H_INTN, and PS0 must be a GPIO because it becomes the WAKE input afterwards.

The clock source is chosen by CLKSEL0 and CLKSEL1 at reset: a 32.768 kHz crystal (CLKSEL0 low or open), an external clock on pin 27 (both high), or the internal oscillator (CLKSEL0 high, CLKSEL1 low or open). The internal oscillator must not be used with either UART mode; I2C and SPI tolerate it. The environmental sensor bus pins ENV_SCL and ENV_SDA need pull-ups even when no sensor is fitted.

Pin hooks¶

Pin Port hook What the driver does with it
NRST SetReset(bool) Pulses it low at Begin() and HardReset(); without it the driver asks the running hub for its advertisement
H_INTN IntAsserted() Reads only when it is low (I2C, SPI); on UART it is optional
H_INTN edge TakeIntTimestamp(uint32_t&) Optional: the time of the edge, for exact sample timestamps
PS0/WAKE SetWake(bool) SPI: pulls it low before a write
BOOTN SetBootn(bool) Optional: held high during the reset sequence

Timing¶

Parameter Value Driver
NRST pulse at least 10 ns Config::reset_pulse_us (default 1 ms)
Internal initialisation after reset t1 at least 90 ms, then t2 about 4 ms Config::startup_timeout_ms bounds each wait
H_INTN after WAKE (SPI) 150 µs typical Config::wake_timeout_us
Host answer to H_INTN within about 10 ms, or the hub gives up and raises it again Poll() should run every few milliseconds (Transports)
UART-SHTP host bytes at least 100 µs apart The driver paces them; or your port does
SPI clock mode 3, up to 3 MHz Your port
I2C clock 100 or 400 kHz; the hub stretches the clock Your port

Supplies¶

VDD 2.4 V to 3.6 V powers the sensors; VDDIO 1.7 V to 3.6 V powers the core and the I/O (the pin table says 1.65 V; the operating-conditions table, which the driver's documentation follows, says 1.7 V). VDD must be on before or together with VDDIO. Put 100 nF at each supply pin and 100 nF from CAP to GND.
