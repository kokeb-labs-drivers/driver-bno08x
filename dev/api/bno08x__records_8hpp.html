Detailed Description

Typed codecs of the FRS records whose layout the documents give (RM S4), the 24 mounting quaternions of the data sheet and the calibration procedure helper.

Every codec turns a record into its 32-bit words and back, with the Q points of the reference manual. Records whose layout is proprietary (DCD, static calibration, simple calibration configuration) or not yet transcribed (the detector configurations RM S4.3.13 to S4.3.20) stay raw words: read and write them with Bno08x::ReadFrs() and Bno08x::WriteFrs().
